What this review proves, and what remains open

Perception JSON → explicit filtering → planner: all plans were generated by the Python planner. This page does not rerun detection, train a network or execute a robot.

The two-group 50/50 recipe and score thresholds are provisional team choices. The score is a heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Relative groups and IDs are scoped to this image; they do not establish tracking across captures.

Group centers use full CIELAB, including chromaticity. These labels are not absolute dark/medium/light material classes. Other brick faces have not been identified. Independent pallet validation is pending.
