Product flow and delivery

Finite-batch rates include fill and drain. Accepted stock and a partial pallet are not counted as shipped product. Quarantine preserves uncertainty; it is not a human review dependency.

Mixing quality and inventory

One product per run. The equal-third recipe is a team assumption. Source orientation and 1.8 kg mass remain provisional. No labor-savings or headcount-removal claim is made.

Target and capacity gap

Selected scenario estimates; not measured factory output. Adding cells alone does not remove the shared handling and inventory constraints.

Cell handling activity

Router and reserve share one physical shuttle per cell. Activity counts executed travel and dwell, clipped at the end of the run. Waiting for resources is excluded. High activity alone does not prove the sole bottleneck.

Engineering basis

Envelope checks use published reference limits. They do not qualify the complete installation. Custom row shuttles, selectors, layer retention, thermal conditions and lift integration require engineering validation.
