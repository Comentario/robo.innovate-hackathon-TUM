Engineering evidence & reference equipment

This is a kinematic, discrete-event concept. Motion profiles, speeds and timings are declared assumptions, not commissioning measurements. Camera imagery and classifications shown here come from the virtual scene. No real image inference runs in this viewer.

ZPA zones use motor-driven rollers and sensors rather than one pneumatic stop per brick. One controller serves up to four zones. Counts below distinguish recorded inspection controllers from the additional collector hardware shown in the scene. Collector controller quantities are planning provisions, not a completed electrical design. Transfer axes and pallet logistics are additional.

Transfer design target: exposed clearance ≤5 mm and level mismatch ≤2 mm for individual bricks. These are engineering targets, not measured installation tolerances. Bridge stiffness, abrasive surfaces, brick edges, clamp force and acceleration need physical testing. Guide rails constrain yaw; they do not simulate friction or certify product stability.
